Rectifier Lab

Diode and thyristor rectifiers, 1φ and 3φ, half and full wave, with an R-L load. 50 Hz, ideal devices, steady-state time-domain simulation.

Developed by U.R.Samuel Paul (24EE10106)

Schematic

Orange = path carrying current right now; moving dashes show the current direction. Drag the slider or press Play.

Parameters

Load = R + L + E in series. E>0 opposes the current (battery charger); E<0 drives it (regenerating DC machine), which is what allows inverter operation for α>90°.

Waveforms

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset. All graphs share one time axis. Zoom out past 360° to see repeated cycles.

Ideal devices and source: no source inductance, commutation overlap, commutation failure (real inverters must keep α below about 150°) or device drops. Thyristors use a wide gate pulse, so they fire when first forward-biased after α. 50 Hz.
